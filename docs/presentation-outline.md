# Project Presentation Outline — WaterWatch SL

## Slide 1 — Title

WaterWatch SL: Community Water-Point Repair Prioritisation and Safe-Alternative Referral System.

## Slide 2 — The Sierra Leone problem

Many communities face unreliable or unsafe water access. Infrastructure limits, seasonal shortages, and failed wells or pumps can increase collection time and exposure to unsafe sources.

## Slide 3 — Proposed solution

A low-bandwidth design for recording reports, scoring urgency, selecting safer alternatives, and preparing referrals.

## Slide 4 — Inputs and outputs

Inputs: location, source, fault, affected people, downtime, safety risk, alternatives. Outputs: score, priority, recommended alternative, referral summary.

## Slide 5 — Algorithm

Validate → score four factors → classify → rank alternatives → create referral → repeat if needed.

## Slide 6 — Flowchart and modules

Show the native Flowgorithm flowchart and explain intake, validation, scoring, priority, alternative selection, and referral modules.

## Slide 7 — Open source, DPG, and SDGs

MIT License, reusable design, privacy-aware workflow, SDG 6 with links to SDG 3 and SDG 10.

## Slide 8 — Expected impact and limitations

Potentially faster, more consistent repair referrals. Weights must be validated locally; the design does not replace engineering inspection or public-health advice.
